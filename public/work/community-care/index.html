Community Care

We serve entire communities through a range of life-changing initiatives — with dignity at the centre of every one.

Fund community care

Who we walk alongside

Catherine's own dream was to understand each person's pain in a personalised way. These programmes carry that forward:

  • Differently-Abled Empowerment (DAEP) — a holistic development plan for the differently-abled.
  • Cancer Warriors' Care — food, shelter, essentials and financial aid for those battling cancer.
  • Widows Welfare — monthly groceries and emotional care for widows affected by addiction, illness or lack of medical aid.
  • Senior Citizens' Support — monthly groceries and essential care for abandoned and vulnerable elders, with dignity and comfort in their golden years.
  • Healthy Grains Programme — nutritious food essentials for families and children in need.
  • Wheelchair donations & Housing Initiative — mobility, shelter, and the strength that comes with stability.

Home is life

Through the Housing Initiative we build strength, stability and self-reliance through shelter — because a safe home is where every second chance begins.
